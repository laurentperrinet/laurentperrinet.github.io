abstract: This paper presents for the first time the embedded stand-alone version
  of the bio-inspired M2APix (Michaelis-Menten auto-adaptive pixels) sensor as a ventral
  optic flow sensor to endow glider-type unmanned aerial vehicles with autonomous
  landing ability. Assuming the aircraft is equipped with any reliable speed measurement
  system such as a global positioning system or an inertial measurement unit, we can
  use the velocity of the glider to determine with high precision its height while
  landing. This information is robust to different outdoor lighting conditions and
  over different kinds of textured ground, a crucial property to control the landing
  phase of the aircraft.
