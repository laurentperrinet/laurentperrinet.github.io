#  "Design of bio-inspired spiking neural networks (spiking neurons) for event-based stereovision" (PhD position, 2020-09 / 2023-09)

* The objective of the work is to design and implement Spiking-Neural-Network-based machine learning methods to extract vision features from the scene from stereo event-based cameras. This PhD takes place within the SPARKS-MS&N team of i3S laboratory (CNRS), as part of the European CHIST-ERA APROVIS3D project. 

* [APROVIS3D grant (ANR-19-CHR3-0008-03)](https://laurentperrinet.github.io/post/2020-06-30_phd-position/)

* Keywords: Vision, Spiking Neural Networks, Bio-Inspired Computer Vision

* Co-supervisors : Jean Martinet (i3S) & Laurent Perrinet (Institut de Neurosciences de la Timone)
