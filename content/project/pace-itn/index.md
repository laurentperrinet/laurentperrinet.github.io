The [PACE ITN](http://itn-pace.eu/) project involves over 50 researchers spread across 10 full and 5 associated partners, from academia and the private sector, established in 7 different European and Associated countries, the PACE network gathers a broad range of expertise from experimental psychology, cognitive neurosciences, brain imaging, technology and clinical sciences.

The PACE Project has received funding from the European Union’s Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme under the Marie Sklodowska-Curie grant agreement No 642961
