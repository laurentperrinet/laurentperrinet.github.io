## Convolutional Neural Networks : Mathematics

* One-dimensional discrete convolution (eg in time) with a kernel f of radius $K$:
$$
(f \ast g)[n]=\sum_{m=-K}^{K} f[m] g[n-m]
$$

---

## Convolutional Neural Networks : Mathematics

* Discrete convolution of an image (two-dimensional):

$$
(f \ast g)[x, y] = \sum_{i=-K}^{K} \sum_{j=-K}^{K} f[i, j] g[i-x, j-y]
$$

---

## Convolutional Neural Networks : Mathematics

{{< figure src="https://stanford.edu/~shervine/teaching/cs-230/illustrations/convolution-layer-a.png" title="[[Amidi & Amidi](https://stanford.edu/~shervine/teaching/cs-230/cheatsheet-convolutional-neural-networks)]" width="90%" >}}

---

## Convolutional Neural Networks : Mathematics

* Discrete convolution of an image on several output channels:

$$
(f \ast g)[x, y, k] = \sum_{i=-K}^{K} \sum_{j=-K}^{K} f[k, i, j, k] g[i-x, j-y]
$$

---

## Convolutional Neural Networks : Mathematics

* Discrete convolution of a multi-channel (e.g. RGB) image on multiple output channels (note [the order of the indices](https://pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.nn.Conv2d.html)):

$$
(f \ast g)[x, y, k] = \\
\sum_{i=-K}^{K} \sum_{j=-K}^{K} \sum_{c=1}^{C} f[k, c, i, j] g[i-x, j-y, c]
$$
