|------------------|---------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| Human eye        | 100  dB | 300 (?) fps | 100 (?) Mpx | 10 mW |

| Ultra-high speed | 64   dB | 10^4 fps    | 0.3--4  Mpx | 300 W |
